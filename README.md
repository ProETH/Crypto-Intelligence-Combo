# Crypto Intelligence Combo

A modular AI Trading Skill that helps AI Agents retrieve, verify, explain, and organize cryptocurrency and exchange information from the appropriate sources.

Instead of relying on one fixed strategy, Crypto Intelligence Combo gives the Agent multiple specialized capabilities for answering crypto-related questions accurately and consistently.

## Capabilities

- Announcements and platform updates
- Listings and delistings
- Spot trading information
- Futures information
- Trading bots and Grid strategies
- Trading fees and limits
- Deposits and withdrawals
- P2P markets
- Earn and staking products
- Airdrops and promotions
- Wallets and supported networks
- Market data
- Token and project information
- Crypto news
- Security and account information
- Crypto research and comparisons

## Core Logic

The Agent first identifies the type of question, then selects the appropriate capability and data source.

The Skill prioritizes current and official information when available, checks dates and conditions, cross-checks important information, and clearly separates confirmed facts from assumptions or analysis.

## Example

User:

"What are the current conditions for Spot Grid trading on CoinW?"

The Agent should:

1. Identify the request as a Trading Bot / Spot Grid query.
2. Find the relevant current information.
3. Check fees, limits, supported assets, and important conditions.
4. Verify the information is current.
5. Provide a concise answer with supporting sources.

## Design Principles

- Modular
- Source-aware
- Current-information focused
- Explainable
- Platform-agnostic
- Agent-friendly
- No unsupported assumptions

## Risk Notice

Crypto information, trading conditions, fees, limits, and platform policies can change.

The Skill should not invent unavailable information and should clearly state when information cannot be verified.

This Skill provides information and research assistance and does not constitute financial advice.
